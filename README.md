# 🛒 Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard | Power BI


## 📌 Project Overview

The **Blinkit Sales Analysis Dashboard** is my first Power BI project, developed to analyze sales performance and understand business trends through interactive data visualization.

This project transforms raw sales data into meaningful insights by analyzing total sales, customer transactions, product quantities, payment methods, monthly sales trends, and city-wise product performance.

The dashboard provides an interactive view of business performance, helping identify sales patterns, product demand, and customer purchasing behavior.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- 📊 Analyze overall sales performance.
- 💰 Track total sales and transaction volume.
- 📦 Identify product-wise quantity and demand.
- 📅 Understand monthly sales trends.
- 💳 Analyze payment methods and sales distribution.
- 🏙️ Explore city-wise product performance.
- 🔍 Use interactive filters for detailed analysis.

## 📈 Dashboard Preview

The Power BI dashboard includes:

- **Total Sales:** Overall sales performance.
- **Total Transactions:** Number of sales transactions.
- **Total Quantity:** Total products sold.
- **Payment Type Analysis:** Sales distribution by payment methods.
- **Monthly Sales Trend:** Month-wise sales performance.
- **City-wise Product Analysis:** Product distribution across cities.
- **Product-wise Quantity:** Identification of high-demand products.
- **Sales Channel Analysis:** Store, Online, and Direct sales comparison.
